# Original paper: polished submission version

This version preserves the method, verified results, and 12-page main-paper
scope of the original NEST submission while improving reader-facing clarity and
page composition.

## Changes

- Replaced defensive statements with direct evidence-backed conclusions.
- Explained the local modules `A`, `B`, `C`, and their shared parent `P` at
  first use.
- Defined the beam and subset dynamic program in plain language.
- Rewrote the opening motivation to explain exactly how an early merge can
  remain uncorrected.
- Positioned PERCH and GRINCH as related rotation/grafting methods while
  distinguishing their linkage or purity goals from structural entropy.
- Tightened float spacing and enabled strict widow/orphan control.
- Kept all sealed numerical claims unchanged.

## Version boundary

The original-polished paper remains an unsupervised structural-entropy method.
GPU/batched search, graft moves, additional hierarchical objectives,
semi-supervised constraints, and targeted repairs for the five residual exact
audit cases are reserved for the separate theory/generalization paper.

The frozen fallback submission is available at commit `d78f2ac` and is not
overwritten by the polished package.
