## What this is
The standardized SE library released with the survey: compute SE, optimize it, and benchmark SE methods
vs classical baselines under one API. This is the **link to share with people who want to USE SE**.
Its validated optimizers (flat `se_louvain`/`se_optimize_fixed_k`, hierarchical `htree.build_tree`,
attributed `segnn`) match/improve published methods on their own objectives (reported in the survey).

## STATUS: released, stable (v0.2.0). Public.
- Install: `pip install -e .` (core) / `pip install -e ".[extra]"` (leidenalg/igraph/infomap baselines).
- Key modules: `calc.py`/`metrics.py` (SE formulas — `structural_entropy_2d` is the reference the
  graph-SE spinoff validates against to 1e-7), `seopt.py` (flat optimizers), `htree.py` (hierarchical;
  NOTE a known parent-pointer bug in `dasgupta_tree` — see graph-SE d5_hierarchy notes), `segnn.py`
  (differentiable SE-GNN), `methods/` (self-registering algorithms), `benchmark.py`.
- Practitioner guide: `docs/USING_SE.md` — "what works, what doesn't" (regime table + traps). Linked from README.
