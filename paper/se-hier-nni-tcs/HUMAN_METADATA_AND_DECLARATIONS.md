# Human metadata and declarations

These items cannot be inferred by an agent. Resolve them before uploading the
journal package.

## Author metadata

- Final author names and order
- Institutional affiliations and postal addresses
- Corresponding author and email
- ORCIDs, if used
- Whether TCS review requires names in the submitted manuscript

## Competing interests

If accurate and approved by every author, Elsevier's standard no-conflict
wording can be used:

> The authors declare that they have no known competing financial interests or
> personal relationships that could have appeared to influence the work
> reported in this paper.

Otherwise, disclose the relevant interests instead.

## Funding

Insert exact funder names and grant numbers, or confirm that the work received
no specific grant. Do not use a no-funding sentence without author approval.

## AI assistance

The manuscript currently proposes this accurate disclosure immediately before
the references:

> During manuscript preparation, the authors used OpenAI Codex to assist with
> manuscript organization, language editing, code review, and mechanical
> consistency checks. The authors reviewed and edited all generated material,
> independently checked the proofs, references, code, and numerical results,
> and take full responsibility for the content of the article.

All authors must approve or revise this wording.
